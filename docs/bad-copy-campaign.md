# BAD COPY — CAMPAIGN SCRIPT
### Twenty-one sols, mission by mission, v0.3

---

## CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE

Stated once, on sol 1, and never restated:

> **Ash Company will hold the Noctis Cistern and the Chain relay line through the dust season — twenty-one sols — until the Compact convoy reaches the works.**

Everything else in this document is noise generated on top of that sentence. The sentence is also a lie, and the player will not be able to prove it until sol 17.

**Honesty tiers are marked for the designer. They are never shown to the player.**

---
---

# ACT I — LEARNING TO READ
### Sols 1–7 · Comms mostly CLEAR · All FRAGOs STRAIGHT

The player learns four people. Nobody important dies unless the player is careless, and if the player is careless, someone should.

---

## SOL 1 — "WAKE"
**Comms:** CLEAR throughout · **Nodes:** Bastion, Lower Chain, Marker 2

```
FRAGO 01 — FROM DIVISION                          [STRAIGHT]
Ash Company assumes responsibility for the Chain
from Bastion to Marker 4 as of 0500.

Walk the line. Nothing more than that today.
Report anything that isn't where the survey says
it should be.

No contact expected. If you get contact, say so
and we'll move on it.
```

**Beats:**
- Turn 1: Yarrow reports in. Flat, complete, three lines. The player learns the report shape on the calmest possible document.
- Turn 2: Rivas reports movement at Marker 2 — *"heat for a dozen, maybe more, and it's sitting wrong"*. Balogun reports the same node: *"Four. Survey team. Call it civilian."* **CONFLICT** fires. Both are survivable choices.
- Turn 3: Whichever the player believed, the suspected-trait line appears in grey: *"You may be starting to learn how Rivas sees things."*
- Turn 4: It was six. Deep Cities engineers, unarmed, working on a tap. They withdraw. Nobody fires. The player never learns the true number.

**Salk, in the room, after:** *"Kestrel's your eyes and she's wrong about everything. You'll want to know by how much. I'd start a tally."*

---

## SOL 2 — "THE CHAIN"
**Comms:** CLEAR → RELAYED · Teaches the Chain, relay beacons, and comms as terrain

```
FRAGO 02 — FROM DIVISION                          [STRAIGHT]
Tower 6 has been down since 0200. Engineers won't
move without an escort and they're not wrong.

Escort the party, hold the ground while they work,
come home. Two hours on site, no more — after that
the light goes and so does your margin.

You have both beacons. Spend them if you need to.
Ask for more and you'll get an argument, not a no.
```

**Beats:**
- Tower 6 sits in a graben. Taking the direct route puts the escort DARK for four turns. Taking the ridge route keeps them CLEAR and costs six.
- Balogun mentions 2nd Battalion working forty kilometres east — a detail line that didn't need to be there. **Thread beat 1.**
- If the player deploys a beacon: it works, and the emitter appears on the board as a bright fixed point that will be there for the whole campaign, visible to everyone.

---

## SOL 3 — "HALLORAN CUT"
**Comms:** CLEAR · The four-report mission · **The design's proof**

```
FRAGO 03 — FROM DIVISION                          [STRAIGHT]
The crest above Halloran Cut is held and it overlooks
your relay line. That's not sustainable.

Take it or suppress it — your call which. Second
Battalion crosses east of you at 0600 and would
appreciate the quiet.

Mortars are yours 0530 to 0700. After that they go
back to Second Battalion, no extension. If you can't
hold it, say so now and we'll move them.
```

*Design note: this is the honest-order template. Names who benefits. Names the support and its limit. Offers an exit.*

**Ground truth (never shown):** reinforced picket, 30 effective, dug eight days, running thermal decoys along the crest.

**The four reports arrive in this order — 0409 Yarrow (stale, correct), 0411 Balogun, 0412 Rivas, 0413 Calloway.** Full text in the Writer's Bible §2. The newest, most specific, only-CERTAIN report is the one that gets people killed.

**Beats:**
- If the player follows Calloway: Harrow assaults thirty dug-in troops. Costly at best, Broken at worst. Calloway survives; two of hers don't. **This is the intended first loss.**
- If the player follows Rivas: no assault, crest stays enemy, Chain degrades for the rest of Act I. Nobody dies. The player has been correct and punished for it, which they need to feel once.
- If the player follows Yarrow: correct, but his report landed at 0440 and the mortar window closed at 0700 — they'll make it with minutes to spare and no margin.
- Balogun does not mention Tsai and Merrick. Telemetry flatlined at 0402. **The single screen that teaches the whole game.**

**Rivas's trait confirms here.** Sixty reported, thirty actual. The grey question mark goes white.

---

## SOL 4 — "PILGRIM'S STAIR"
**Comms:** RELAYED · Vehicle escort · Teaches the cost of splitting

```
FRAGO 04 — FROM DIVISION                          [STRAIGHT]
Water train, eleven vehicles, Bastion to the Cistern
via the Stair. It goes tonight because it goes every
fourth night and they'll know that.

You'll want to be on the Stair before the train is.
Detach if you have to — I'd rather you were thin in
two places than late in one.

Calloway has the route survey. She asked for it.
```

**Beats:**
- The DETACH verb becomes necessary for the first time. Detached fireteams report separately and thinly.
- Calloway's file surfaces: Staff, Pavonis, exemplary, zero combat commands. **Thread beat 1.**
- The train is not attacked. The tension is entirely the player's, generated by four contradictory reports about a road where nothing happens. Act I needs exactly one mission like this or the player never learns that fear is also a filter.

---

## SOL 5 — "COLD HARBOUR"
**Comms:** CLEAR → DARK (player's choice) · First voluntary blindness

```
FRAGO 05 — FROM DIVISION                          [STRAIGHT]
There's a dead pumping station at Cold Harbour that
the survey says has been dead for thirty years and
the thermal says has been warm for six days.

Go and look. Don't get drawn in — if it's held,
it's held, and we'll deal with it as a different
problem.

There is no relay in that graben and there isn't
going to be one. You'll lose them for a while.
Decide now whether you're all right with that.
```

**Beats:**
- Cold Harbour is DARK. Whoever goes in vanishes. Kestrel is the right squad and the player will not want to send her.
- Six turns of nothing but a telemetry strip.
- They come out with the first real intelligence of the campaign: Cold Harbour is a Deep Cities staging point, recently used, currently empty. And a detail nobody asked for — the station has been *maintained*. Somebody has been keeping thirty-year-old pumps alive.
- **If the player deploys a beacon here, flag it.** It matters enormously on sol 19.

---

## SOL 7 — "THE SINK"
**Comms:** DARK · **First SHADED FRAGO** · Act I closes

```
FRAGO 06 — FROM DIVISION                          [SHADED]
Enemy pressure on the Chain is easing. Take advantage
of it.

Clear the Sink and push to Marker 4 by last light.
Resistance expected to be light. Air is available
on request.

Confirm receipt.
```

*Tells: "easing" and "light" are unsourced. "Available on request" is not allocation. No exit clause. The objective is real; the cost is not what they're telling you.*

**Beats:**
- The Sink is permanent DARK, five turns deep, and pressure is not easing.
- Air, on request, is refused. The refusal arrives as one line: *"Air is committed. Continue as ordered."*
- **QUERY is available here for the first time**, and the partial tell it returns is real: *"Assessment is Division's, not the ground's."*
- Expected outcome: Costly. If a squad leader dies in Act I, it is here, and the player will have had two clear opportunities to see it coming.

**Act I closes** with the player holding four confirmed traits, one degraded relay line, and their first genuine suspicion that the orders are not made of the same material as the reports.

# ACT II — THE ORDER FROM INSIDE THE HOUSE
### Sols 8–14 · Comms RELAYED to THIN · Shaded and Rotten enter

Yarrow's rotation lands on sol 9. Hesketh is revealed on sol 14. Intercepts begin. Everything the player learned to rely on gets taken away in a specific order.

---

## SOL 8 — "WATER DISCIPLINE"
```
FRAGO 07 — FROM DIVISION                          [SHADED]
Suit reclaim across the company is running eleven
per cent under establishment. That is a supply
problem and it is being addressed.

In the interim, patrols are to carry standard load
and not standard-plus. Duration unaffected.

This is not a matter for query.
```

**Beats:** It is not being addressed. Every squad now degrades faster on long patrols, which is invisible until sol 15 when it isn't. Salk: *"'Being addressed' means somebody's written it down."*

---

## SOL 9 — "ROTATION"
**The Yarrow decision.** The most important choice in Act II and it is not presented as one.

```
FRAGO 08 — FROM DIVISION                          [STRAIGHT]
Transport at Bastion 0400 sol 10. Sgt. D. Yarrow,
2nd Squad, twenty-two years, rotation processed.

Company is short and we know it. If you need him
held, request it and it'll be granted — nobody
here will make you justify it.

Your call.
```

*Design note: Division being generous is the cruellest thing it does all campaign. There is no rotten framing here. It's a real choice with no correct answer, and it will haunt the player either way.*

**Three outcomes:**

**Let him go.** 2nd Squad passes to Cpl. Rusk — *Green*, one detail line, no recommendation. The player loses their calibration source on schedule, cleanly, and spends Act III unable to check anyone. Yarrow's last transmission:
> *"Anvil's a good squad. Halloran's better than he thinks. That's the whole handover, sir."*

**Hold him.** Nerve −1 immediately and permanently. He never mentions it. His reports get *slower* — the Slow trait deepens by one turn. He survives to sol 16 at best, and when he dies the death card reads *rotation was processed posthumously*, and the player did that.

**Do nothing.** Transport leaves without him because nobody signed anything. Identical to holding him, except Yarrow now believes he was forgotten, and one line in a report on sol 12 tells you so:
> *"Understood. — Didn't hear on the transport. Not asking."*

---

## SOL 10 — "INTERCEPT"
**First enemy transmissions.** Introduced *after* the player has finished learning four filters, so the fifth voice arrives as a destabilisation rather than a tutorial.

```
FRAGO 09 — FROM DIVISION                          [STRAIGHT]
Signals is picking up traffic off the Cut and can't
place the transmitter. You're closer than they are.

Sweep the crest line. If you find it, don't destroy
it — we'd rather listen.
```

**The first three intercepts, in order:**
```
—— clear by first light, they haven't moved off the ridge ——
```
```
—— tell him the count is wrong, the count has been wrong
since —— [cut]
```
```
—— no, leave the heaters running. Let them look. ——
```

*The third explains the decoys and is the most useful line in the act. It is formally indistinguishable from the second, which is bait. The player has no method for telling them apart and never will.*

---

## SOL 11 — "FEMI"
```
FRAGO 10 — FROM DIVISION                          [SHADED]
Second Battalion is in contact east of the Cut and
has requested support. We are not in a position to
send them anyone.

Hold your line. Do not move east.

We appreciate this will read poorly.
```

**Beats:**
- **Balogun thread beat 3.** His brother Femi is in 2nd Battalion. The player learns this from Salk, from a personnel file, at the exact moment they are ordered not to go east.
- Balogun does not ask. He reports normally, three excellent detail lines, all about the ground in front of him.
- **PRESS him toward the east and he goes** — no protest, one word, and it is the clearest possible demonstration of the trap. Nerve −1 and he thanks you.
- If the player holds the line as ordered: Balogun's Nerve drops one step per turn for three turns, silently, and the only thing on screen is the bar.
- The **LEVEL** key unlocks here permanently.

**Salk:** *"Both transfer requests refused. Same signature both times."* *(pause)* *"I'm not telling you what to do with that. I'm telling you it's in the file and you're the only one who reads the file."*

---

## SOL 12 — "THE CISTERN"
```
FRAGO 11 — FROM DIVISION                          [SHADED]
You will move the company to the Cistern works and
hold there.

The Chain north of Marker 4 is to be given up.
Towers 7 through 11 will not be re-manned.

Do not interpret this as a withdrawal.
```

**Beats:** The player gives up half their relay line on order, and every node north of Marker 4 goes permanently THIN or DARK. This is the moment the board goes dim, and it is done by the player's own hand, on instruction, for a reason nobody explains. Act III is unwinnable as an information problem because of this order, and the player agreed to it on sol 12.

**Aduba's line surfaces here**, under Balogun's report, unanswered:
> *[ADUBA, 0655] Sergeant, if I put my real age in, do they send me home or do they charge me?*

---

## SOL 13 — "KESTREL ALONE"
**Comms:** DARK, eleven turns

```
FRAGO 12 — FROM DIVISION                          [SHADED]
We need eyes past Marker 6 and there is no way to
do that and stay in contact.

Send your recon element. Duration is at your
discretion but the answer we need is at least
four grabens deep.

They'll be on their own. That's understood here.
```

**Beats:**
- Eleven turns. Telemetry only. Four heartbeats and nothing else on the screen.
- On turn 7, one flatlines. **Rfn. Bel.** No report, no context, no name card until Kestrel surfaces, because nobody is there to file it.
- **Rivas thread beat 3** arrives when she comes back, in her own words, unprompted, in a report that had no reason to include it:
> *"Eleven days I've been telling you numbers. You should know the file says I was wrong twice and only one of those was true and they never told me which. I've stopped asking. — Marker 6 is held. Sixty, maybe more."*
- It was forty-one.

---

## SOL 14 — "FRAGO 13"
**The rotten one.** Arrives at 0402. The player is DARK until 0447.

```
FRAGO 13 — FROM DIVISION                          [ROTTEN]
   [transmitted 0402 — received 0447]

It has been decided that the Cistern is to be held
through the night.

Withdrawal is not authorised. Adequate support will
be provided. Confirm compliance by 0500.

Query window has closed.
```

*Every tell at once: passive voice with no author, unspecified support, a deadline that expired forty-seven minutes before it was legible, and it was sent into a known DARK window. Somebody chose that hour.*

**The player's three responses matter for the first time.** REFUSE costs Command Standing hard and is survivable. COMPLY costs people. QUERY is impossible, which is the point — and the attempt is what surfaces Hesketh.

**RENFREW, first direct contact:**
> *"You've been querying. That's fine — it's in the manual, it's why the channel exists."* *(pause)* *"But I'd like you to be clear that when you ask me whether an order is necessary, you're asking me to do a sum I have already done, and to show you the working because you'd like to feel better about it. I don't mind. I'd just like us both to know what's happening."*

**Act II closes** with a name on the signature block, half the Chain given away, and a company that has begun to notice that the orders and the ground are describing two different wars.

# ACT III — DUST
### Sols 15–21 · THIN and DARK · The player finishes the campaign half-blind

---

## SOL 15 — "SEASON"
The global dust storm arrives. No FRAGO — Division has nothing to say.

**Permanent from this point:** every comms state degrades one step. CLEAR becomes RELAYED. RELAYED becomes THIN. THIN becomes DARK. The board the player gave away on sol 12 is now unreachable, and the reclaim shortfall from sol 8 becomes visible in every condition track at once.

**Salk:** *"Eleven per cent under, they said. It's nineteen. It was always going to be nineteen."*

---

## SOL 16 — "CALLOWAY'S GROUND"
```
FRAGO 14 — FROM DIVISION                          [SHADED]
The Stair is the only route with load capacity and
it will be needed. Hold it.

Assessment is that the storm is worse for them than
for you.
```

*It isn't. The Deep Cities have lived here for three generations.*

**Beats:**
- Calloway reports the Stair as thinly held. Every observation true. The conclusion is wrong for the fourth time and she is still saying **CERTAIN**.
- **Her first loss happens here** — Rfn. Fen, or whoever remains. It happens because she was confident.
- Her next report is the first four-line document she has ever filed with no recommendation in it.
- **The LEVEL polarity inverts permanently.** From this point the button that destroyed her in Act I is the only one that reaches her.
> *"I don't know what I'm looking at any more. Tell me what to do and I'll do it."* *(pause)* *"That's not me asking you to be certain. I know better now."*

---

## SOL 17 — "THE MANIFEST"
**No FRAGO. Salk finds it herself.**

> *"I pulled the manifest for the convoy."* *(long pause)* *"It isn't a relief column. There's no water plant in it. There's sixty tonnes of shaped charge and a survey team, and the routing has it at the Cistern, not at Bastion."* *(pause)* *"I want you to tell me I've read it wrong."*

**The campaign objective inverts.** Ash Company is not holding the Cistern until relief arrives. Ash Company is holding the Cistern until the demolition team arrives, and the company's presence is what makes the collapse of the aquifer look like a defensive last stand rather than a decision.

They are the alibi. They have been the alibi since sol 1.

**Critically: the player cannot prove this.** A manifest is a document. Salk can suspect. Salk cannot know. Every ending is chosen on incomplete information, exactly like every other decision in the game.

---

## SOL 18 — "QUERY"
The player may spend their entire comms window asking Hesketh directly. Most will.

**RENFREW:**
> *"The aquifer is a hundred and ten billion litres. Spent on the Deep Cities it buys four hundred thousand people about sixty years. Spent on atmosphere it buys everyone who comes after them a planet."* *(pause)* *"You're going to tell me those people are real and the others aren't. You're right. That's the whole of the disagreement, and I've heard it, and my answer is that I'm paid to count the ones who aren't here yet."*

*He does not lie. He is never permitted to lie. The moment he lies, the player is off the hook.*

---

## SOL 19 — "COLD HARBOUR AGAIN"
**The third option unlocks — and only for some players.**

Requires: Company Trust high, **and** a relay beacon deployed at Cold Harbour on sol 5. A player who spent that beacon fourteen sols ago for an unrelated reason now has a channel into the Deep Cities network. A player who didn't, doesn't, and there is no way to build one now.

**ROQUE:**
> *"This is Roque. I'm told you're the one who's been listening."* *(pause)* *"I'm not going to ask you to surrender and you're not going to ask me. I want to know whether you've read the convoy manifest, and I want you to know that if you have, and you sit on it, there are four hundred thousand people who will find out eventually who you were."*

---

## SOL 20 — "THE CONVOY"
It appears on the board at last light, on schedule, exactly as promised for twenty sols. Sixty tonnes of shaped charge and a survey team.

Every squad leader still alive files a report about it. Nobody says what it is. Balogun counts the vehicles. Rivas says there are more than there are. Halloran files one line. Calloway, if she lives, writes: *"That is not a water plant. I don't know what it is. I'm not certain."*

---

## SOL 21 — "BAD COPY"
The final window. One order. Three doors.

| | What it does |
|---|---|
| **HOLD** | The company holds. The convoy reaches the Cistern. The objective is achieved. |
| **WITHDRAW** | The company pulls back to Bastion. The convoy turns around — it cannot work an unsecured site. Objective failed. |
| **TRANSMIT** | Send the manifest to Roque on the Cold Harbour channel. Treason under Compact law, and the only door that stops the demolition permanently. |

# ENDINGS

Flat, administrative, filed by someone who wasn't there and won't read it. They should land like a slap and never editorialise.

---

### HOLD — objective achieved
> **AFTER ACTION — NOCTIS CISTERN, SOLS 1–21**
>
> The convoy reached the works at 0340 on the twenty-first sol. The Cistern was held throughout. Divisional assessment is that the operation met its objective in full.
>
> Company strength on relief was [N] effective from an establishment of twenty-two. Replacements were drawn from theatre reserve on [N] occasions.
>
> Survey and demolition were completed on sol 24. The Noctis aquifer is assessed as non-recoverable. Atmospheric allocation has been revised upward by four per cent.
>
> Ration in the Deep Cities was reduced to two litres on sol 40 and has not been restored.
>
> No further action is recommended.

---

### WITHDRAW — objective failed, company intact
> **AFTER ACTION — NOCTIS CISTERN, SOLS 1–21**
>
> The company withdrew to Bastion at 0410 on the twenty-first sol without authorisation. The convoy was unable to secure the site and returned to Pavonis.
>
> Company strength on relief was [N] effective. The commanding officer has been relieved and is returned to Pavonis pending review.
>
> The Cistern was retaken by Compact forces on sol 34 and the survey completed on sol 41.
>
> The delay is assessed at twenty sols.
>
> *Hesketh, appended by hand:* "For what it's worth, Major — I don't think you're wrong. I think you're early, and being early is the same as being wrong when there's a convoy on the road."

---

### TRANSMIT — the treason ending
> **BOARD OF INQUIRY — PRELIMINARY FINDING**
>
> On sol 21 an encrypted transmission originating from Bastion ops was received at Pavonis Deep. The transmission contained convoy manifest DR-4471 in full.
>
> Deep Cities forces occupied the Noctis works at 0900 sol 22. The survey column was turned at the Stair without engagement. Demolition has not taken place and is not currently practicable.
>
> The aquifer remains in production. Ration in the Deep Cities was restored to six litres on sol 30.
>
> The officer commanding Ash Company has been relieved, detained, and charged. The company itself is assessed as having had no knowledge and has been redistributed across 2nd and 6th Battalions.
>
> *Salk, in the room, before they came for you:* "Give me the order in writing and I'll transmit it. You're not doing that part alone."

---

### Company modifiers — appended to any of the above

**Gutted** (fewer than eight effective):
> *Appended:* Of the twenty-two soldiers on establishment at sol 1, [N] were killed. Of the fourteen replacements drawn from theatre reserve, [N] were killed. The company is assessed as combat-ineffective and will be reconstituted.

**Mutinied** (Company Trust bottomed out):
> *Appended:* Two squads did not acknowledge orders after sol 19 and were subsequently located at Cold Harbour, intact, having taken no further part. No charges have been preferred. It is not considered to be in anyone's interest to establish precisely what occurred.

**If Yarrow was held past his rotation:**
> *Appended:* Sgt. D. Yarrow's rotation was processed posthumously. His effects were forwarded to the address on file.

**If Aduba survived to sol 21:**
> *Appended:* A discrepancy has been identified in the enlistment record of Rfn. S. Aduba, 1st Squad. The matter has been referred and is not expected to be pursued.

## THE LAST RULE

There is no post-campaign screen that reveals what was actually on those ridges. Not a codex, not a statistics page, not an unlockable. The player finishes the game not knowing how many there were at Halloran Cut, and if they want to argue about it afterwards, that argument is the product.
